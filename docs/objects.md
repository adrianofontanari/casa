# Objects in the room

Click an object to open its panel.

## Banner: today's goal

Shows the goal from today's daily note. Click it, type, and press Enter to save it in today's note. The formats Casa reads and writes are in the [README](../README.md#how-casa-reads-and-writes-your-notes).

## Agenda: tasks

An open notebook. The left page has today's goal and today's tasks; the right page has the next days, one tab per day (up to 7 days ahead). Tick a task and it is ticked in your note. You can also add a task, edit a task's text, and open the note a task comes from.

If the note changed in Obsidian since the panel read it, nothing is written: the panel reloads and tells you.

## Wall calendar

Today's events, with the next one written on the page. Click it for the whole week as a time grid. The calendar is read-only: Casa never changes your calendar. Setup: [Calendar](calendar.md).

## Window: weather, air and webcam

The real weather where you live, drawn in the window. Click it for:

- the weather now, the next hours and five days;
- the air quality outside, and at home if you set a sensor link;
- one webcam strip. The default is the NOAA BuoyCAM on station 46026, off San Francisco: six views around the buoy side by side, which you scroll sideways. At night the views are dark, and when the camera is offline the window says so.

Each part loads on its own: if one source fails, the others stay. Weather and air need a city.

## Seasons and light

The room follows your computer's clock and the date: the light changes through the day and the room changes with the season. Nothing to set.

## TV

Nine hand-drawn channels: the clock, the weather, and seven little scenes (a lighthouse, snowy pines, the moon over a field, a city at night, the sea at dusk, rain on the glass, a fireplace). Turn the knob for the next one. In calm mode the channels show still frames.

## Typewriter: quotes

The paper shows a new quote every hour. Click it to read the quote big, roll another, or add yours. Quotes come from `Casa/Quotes.md`; until you add one, Casa uses 20 built-in public-domain quotes.

## Headphones: music and sounds

Clicking the headphones opens the player. Pick a lofi track, or an ambient sound: sea, rain, fire or birds. Closing the panel keeps the sound playing; your last choice and the volume are remembered.

Ambient sounds are made on your computer. A lofi track is downloaded from this repository's GitHub release `music-v1` only when you press play. With **Music** off in settings, the player says so and plays nothing.

## Cork board: post-its

Click the board to open your post-its on a dotted page. Pick a colour (yellow, pink, blue or green) and write a new one, edit one, drag it to move it, or delete it (with undo). They are saved in `Casa/Post-its.md`.

## Power strip: battery

Your computer's battery level and whether it is charging. Read on your computer, no network.

## Buddy

Pebble, a small penguin, keeps you company with a friendly line for the time of day. Its panel has a switch to hide it, handy before a screen share; **Show buddy** in settings brings it back.

## Chair and backpack: coming soon

These two belong to packs that are not out yet. Clicking one opens a small card: "Coming soon", a line about the pack, and a **Join the waitlist** button. The button opens [the waitlist](https://casa.adrianofontanari.com/#notify) in your browser, only if you click it.

## Put away and bring back

Every object's panel has **Put away** in its title bar, Pebble and Biscuit included: the object leaves the room, with its tag. Putting Pebble away is the same as turning off **Show buddy** in settings. While something is put away, a box sits on the floor, front left: open it to bring objects back one by one, or all together. The yellow "coming soon" tags go away after your first click on each object. Both choices are saved in this vault.
